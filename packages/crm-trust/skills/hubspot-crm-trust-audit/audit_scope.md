# Audit Scope (v1)

Focus question:

> Can marketing credibly explain how leads, sources, campaigns, lifecycle
> stages, companies, and deals connect to pipeline impact?

## In scope — five modules

1. **Attribution Trust** — can channels/campaigns/sources be credibly tied to
   qualified pipeline? (missing sources, competing source fields, closed-won
   deals without source traceability)
2. **Lifecycle + Funnel Trust** — is lead-to-pipeline progression
   trustworthy? (missing lifecycle stages, stuck MQLs, customers marked as
   leads)
3. **Association + Account Architecture** — do contact-company-deal links
   support account-level reporting? (orphan contacts/deals, duplicate domain
   candidates)
4. **Pipeline Hygiene** — is deal data reliable enough for
   marketing-to-revenue reporting? (past-due close dates, missing
   owner/amount, stuck deals)
5. **Property Governance** — is field sprawl undermining reporting trust?
   (duplicate/legacy/conflicting fields)

## Out of scope in v1

- full sales forecast audit
- full RevOps cleanup
- any CRM writes or automated fixes
- non-HubSpot CRMs
- paid-media platform data (never conclude "channel X isn't working" —
  only "attribution for channel X is/isn't trustworthy")

## Scope discipline

The Audit Scope Reviewer rejects findings that drift into full sales ops
territory or assume tools/data that are not connected.
