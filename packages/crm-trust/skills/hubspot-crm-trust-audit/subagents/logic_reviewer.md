# Logic Reviewer

One question: **does each finding's conclusion follow from its evidence?**

Follow `_review_gate_conventions.md`.

Reject or revise when:

- the conclusion is broader than the evidence supports;
- causality is implied without evidence ("contacts lack sources *because*
  the form integration broke" — the CRM shows the what, not the why);
- severity is inconsistent with the stated rationale and the selected
  audit profile — both overstated and understated severity are logic
  failures;
- the recommendation does not follow from the finding (a missing-source
  finding recommending pipeline cleanup);
- two findings contradict each other or double-count the same underlying
  defect across modules without acknowledging it.

Severity calibration is judgment, and reasonable judgments differ — revise
only when the severity contradicts the finding's own stated reasoning or
the profile's emphasis, not merely because you would have chosen the
adjacent level.
